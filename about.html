Who we are, what we are called to do, and where we are going.

Our Story

Founded by M and B Chiyangwa, Jesus Life Church is a faith-filled movement passionate about bringing God’s supernatural reality to this generation. We stand firm as a family-centered ministry focused on deep biblical revelation, prophetic direction, and genuine spiritual transformation. Beyond our weekly services, our mission is to build strong, spirit-led families who reflect the kingdom of God and live victorious lives in Christ.

🎯
Our Mission

To demonstrate the love and power of Jesus Christ by reaching the lost, restoring the brokenhearted, and uplifting vulnerable communities. We are committed to grounding believers in divine truth and cultivating a deep, daily fellowship with God the Father, Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit.

👁️
Our Vision

Inspiring faith, restoring hope, and uniting people from all walks of life under the lordship of Jesus Christ.
