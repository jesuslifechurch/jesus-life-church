Privacy Notice

How Jesus Life Church looks after the details you share on this website.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through our forms:

  • Giving: your name, phone number, email (optional), what you are giving toward, the amount, and your payment details such as the number you paid from.
  • I’m New and prayer requests: your name, phone number, email (optional) and your message.
  • Testimonies: your testimony, your name, and your phone number. Only the testimony and the name option you choose are ever shown publicly, and only after we approve it.
  • Store requests: your name, phone number, what you would like to order, and delivery notes. We do not take payments on this website.
  • One-on-one appointment requests: your name, phone number, email (optional), topic, preferred time and a short note. Please keep notes brief and do not write sensitive details.
  • Speak Up messages: only the message and category you send. We do not ask for or save your name, phone number or device details. If you choose to leave a contact so we can reply, we will have it.
  • Messages by WhatsApp or email that you send us directly.

Why we collect it

To confirm your gifts and give you a receipt, to follow up with visitors, to pray with you, and to share testimonies you have allowed us to share. We do not sell your information.

Where it is kept

Your details are stored in private Google Sheets that only the church’s authorised administrators can open. Please do not send card numbers, PINs or passwords through this website. We will never ask for them.

Who can see it

Only a small number of church administrators and, for prayer requests marked private, the prayer team. Public pages never show phone numbers or email addresses.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to correct or delete your details or a testimony you sent, by messaging us on WhatsApp or emailing jesuslifechurchharare@gmail.com.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your Bible reading plan progress, sermon notes and store basket are saved only on your own device.

Last updated: October 2026. This is a plain-language notice prepared for the church. It is not legal advice, so please have it reviewed to make sure it meets Zimbabwe’s data protection requirements.
